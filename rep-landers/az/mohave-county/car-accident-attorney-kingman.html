What to Do After a Car Accident in Kingman

Short on time? Do these, then call us. We handle the rest.

  1. Call 911 and get checked out. Go to the ER or urgent care, even if you feel okay.
  2. Take photos. Cars, plates, the scene, your injuries. Get witness names.
  3. Get the crash report. It's usually filed by city police, the Mohave County Sheriff or Arizona DPS. We can get it for you.
  4. Don't give a recorded statement to the other driver's insurance company.
  5. Keep every bill and receipt, and track the days of work you miss.
  6. Talk to a lawyer before you sign anything. A release ends your claim.

How long do I have to file a claim in Arizona?

Usually two years from the crash to file a lawsuit, under A.R.S. §12-542.2 Claims against a city, county or the state require a notice of claim within 180 days (A.R.S. §12-821.01). Call early. VERIFY legal review

What if I was partly at fault?

Arizona uses pure comparative fault. You can recover even if you were mostly at fault; your award is reduced by your share (A.R.S. §12-2505).3 VERIFY legal review

What if the other driver had no insurance?

Your own uninsured/underinsured motorist coverage may pay. Arizona doesn't use PIP no-fault coverage, so the at-fault driver's insurer is usually the main source. VERIFY legal review
