Inside Knowledge

We know the insurance playbook. We learned it from the inside.

Founding partner George Goldberg spent his first two years in practice defending airlines and insurance companies in injury litigation, before switching sides in 1996. He learned every delay tactic and every lowball script. When the adjuster calls with a low offer, he already knows their next three moves.

Tactic 01

The fast lowball

An early offer arrives before you know what your injuries will cost. Signing usually ends your claim for good — even if your injuries get worse.

Tactic 02

The recorded statement

Friendly questions on a recorded line. What you say can be used to cut your claim.

Tactic 03

The long delay

Insurers profit by dragging cases out, hoping you'll take less just to be done.

Tactic 04

The blame shift

They argue you were partly at fault to shrink what they owe. Don't accept their version without a review.
