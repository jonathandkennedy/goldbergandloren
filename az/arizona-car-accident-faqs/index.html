Frequently Asked Questions

How long do I have to file a car accident claim in Arizona?

Usually two years from the crash to file a lawsuit (A.R.S. §12-542).2 Claims against a city, county or the state need a notice of claim within 180 days (A.R.S. §12-821.01).

What if I was partly at fault?

Arizona uses pure comparative fault: your recovery is reduced by your share of fault, but not barred (A.R.S. §12-2505).3

Is Arizona a no-fault state?

No. Arizona is an at-fault state, so the at-fault driver's liability insurer usually pays. Your own uninsured/underinsured motorist coverage may also apply.

Does Arizona cap injury damages?

The Arizona Constitution bars laws that limit damages for injury or death (Ariz. Const. art. 2, §31).
